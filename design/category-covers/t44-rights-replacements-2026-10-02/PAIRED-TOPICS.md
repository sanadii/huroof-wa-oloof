Each source is 1,254 × 1,254 PNG. Sharp resized the originals to centered 320 × 320 and 640 × 640 WebP at quality 82, fully decoding outputs afterward. The four source images and all sixteen derivative files are identified by the hashes above and can be checked against the current live-category fixture. The previous imported assets remain untouched. The original-generation directions were: (1) globe with invented geometric cloth pennants, avoiding real flags, labels and marks; (2) unlabelled map with pins and a generic mixed skyline, avoiding famous protected structures and printed labels; (3) fictional patterned notes and coins, avoiding denominations and real currency designs; (4) three generic unbranded car types, avoiding badges, plates and copied model signatures. All asked for one centered, legible square photographic-editorial category cover with warm sand, teal and restrained blue/gold accents, and no text, people, logos or watermark.

This is first-pass visual and byte review for a draft preview. It does not certify a real flag/map/currency/car fact, clear all third-party similarity risk, approve the remaining imported covers, establish 300 reviewed questions, or prove hosted/production delivery. Production promotion remains held for exact hosted readback and full human visual/rights acceptance.
