## Cross-page system observations

Observed repeatedly: Arabic-first confidence; near-black stage; large high-contrast headings;
one dominant action per decision; grouped saturated bands; strong framed zones; image/category
breadth; focused live-flow mode; explicit empty/disabled states; consistent shell.

Classic catalogue shell differs from online focus shell: classic supports browsing, search,
chips, catalogue media, match configuration, and persistent selected-count progression;
online collapses context into a deep-navy single-column entry/mode task. The join variation is
light-focused. These are observations, not a prescribed light/dark system. Responsive behavior
is unknown.

## Reusable principles for this product

1. Make the current decision, legal primary action, then explanation/status visible in that
   order; keep disabled reasons and selection count legible in setup.
2. Use a centered, framed shared shell for entry, rules, setup, lobby, results, and admin
   instead of mimicking page composition.
3. Give live host/player/audience a focused operational mode with explicit exit/recovery state;
   the selected product’s version is the history-0005 centered-board midnight broadcast core.
4. Treat category breadth as content information. Tahadani covers remain rights-aware content
   media with title strip and loading/error fallback—not promotional wallpaper.
5. Make empty/search/error/closed/offline/reconnecting state explanatory and actionable.
