# Visual Concepts — Canonical Three-Option Set

Status: closed after human selection  
Date presented: 2026-09-03  
Rule: these are the exact three direction families considered. Add future directions in a new numbered history record; do not rewrite this set.

## 1. `concept-studio-letters` — استوديو الحروف

Primary reference: Sing–Sing.  
Direction: a sunny, minimal Arabic cultural studio with an amber canvas, midnight ink, jade active states, sharp geometry, oversized Arabic typography, and the hex board treated as the main cultural object.  
Best fit: an ownable Gulf/Arabic identity suitable for family play, schools, events, and broadcast.  
Risks: can become generic “warm editorial” design if the hex board, score tension, and active gameplay are not dominant.  
Rejects: beige luxury clichés, decorative calligraphy, gold gradients, rounded SaaS cards, and ornamental Arabesque stock art.

## 2. `concept-play-board` — لوحة اللعب المرحة

Primary reference: Playdate.  
Direction: cheerful and tactile, with warm yellow blocks, charcoal typography, large physical controls, and playful board feedback.  
Best fit: mass-market family play and casual gatherings.  
Risks: can look childish or toy-like and can overcrowd team colors.  
Rejects: 3D plastic effects, excessive colors, violet brand drift, and cartoon decoration.

## 3. `concept-night-arena` — ساحة الألوف

Primary reference: .SWOOSH.  
Direction: dark graphite arena, white type, electric active cues, huge central board, and broadcast-like score pylons.  
Best fit: tournaments, projection, and livestream production.  
Risks: less welcoming for family play and prone to generic gamer styling.  
Rejects: rainbow neon, glassmorphism, glowing everything, cinematic stock photography, and dense esports chrome.

## Human outcome

Selected: `concept-studio-letters` — استوديو الحروف.  
The other two concepts remain evidence of genuine divergence; they are not ingredients to average into the selected design.
